DRAGGING OBJECTS

About the game

The Dragging Objects is a game that develops the user's fine motor skills by challenging the user to find the circle that needs to be moved into a specific targeted location.

How to play

  • Click "Start" to play the game.

  • Drag the yellow circle to the dotted circle on the white screen.

  • There is a timer countdown to 0.

  • At 0, the current score is record.

  • Click on "Try Again" to play new game. Best score is always record.

Purpose

The Dragging Objects supports children to get acquainted with directions.
