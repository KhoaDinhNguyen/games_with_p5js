CLICKING OBJECTS

About the game

The Clicking Objects is a toddler friendly game that allows the user to click/point to the specific object, once the object is clicked by the user it will move to a new random location.

How to play

  • Click "Start" to play the game.

  • Click on the red circle appearing on the white screen.

  • There is a timer countdown to 0.

  • At 0, the current score is record.

  • Click on "Try Again" to play new game. Best score is always record.

Purpose

The licking Objects helps toddlers increase their reflexibility and arms muscle.
