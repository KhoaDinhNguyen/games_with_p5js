DRAWING LINES

About the game

The Drawing Lines is a toddler friendly game that is able to help control fine movements of the user by having to draw over dotted lines.

How to play

  • Click "Start" to play the game.

  • Follow the dot line to finish the drawing.

  • Click "Next" to have more diffcult level.

  • Try to finish all four levels and record the fastest finishes.

  • Click on "Try Again" if draw outsice the dotted line.

Purpose

Drawing Lines is able to grow toddlers skills in being able to have a greater control over their movements by drawing in straight lines
